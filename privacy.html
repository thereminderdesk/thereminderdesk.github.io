Reminder Desk

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Reminder Desk is a small, private service that sends you an email reminder at the time you choose. It's run by an individual for family use, not a company, and it's free. Accounts need an access password shared within the family, and reminders only go to members. This page explains what information it uses, why, and what control you have.

What we collect

  • Your reminders: the reminder text, any notes, place or link you add, the email address to send it to, the date and time, and your time zone. We need these to send the reminder.
  • Account details: your name and email address, confirmed with a one-time code. If you use a password, we store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, never the password itself.
  • WhatsApp, if you connect it: your WhatsApp number and the personal key CallMeBot gives you. The key only lets Reminder Desk send messages to your own number.
  • Google sign-in, if you use it: your name, email address and Google's account ID for you. We don't receive your Google password and we don't get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.
  • Security information: when you sign in, fail to sign in, or reset your password, we record the time, a general device description (for example "Chrome on Android") and your time zone. A random device code stored in your browser lets us notice sign-ins from new devices so we can warn you.

We don't use cookies for tracking, advertising or analytics. Your browser stores a sign-in token and a few preferences on your device so you stay signed in.

How we use it

  • To send your reminders at the time you chose.
  • To run your account: sign-in, password resets and showing your reminders.
  • To keep accounts secure: new-device alerts, blocking repeated failed sign-ins and stopping misuse.
  • To send account emails you'd expect, such as a welcome email, password reset codes and security alerts.

If you receive reminders you don't want, reply to any reminder email and they'll be stopped.

We never sell your information, never show ads, and never use your reminders for anything other than sending them to you.

Where it's stored

Reminder Desk runs on Google's services. Your information is stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive belonging to the Reminder Desk Google account, and emails are sent through Gmail. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. Daily backup copies are kept for 14 days, then removed.

If you connect WhatsApp, your reminders are also delivered through CallMeBot, a free third-party WhatsApp service. To do that, the reminder text and your WhatsApp number pass through CallMeBot and WhatsApp. You can turn WhatsApp off or disconnect it at any time in My reminders.

Who can see it

Reminder Desk is run by one person for family use. That person manages accounts (for example, unlocking an account or stopping misuse) using admin tools that show account and security details only, not the content of your reminders.

Because the information is stored in that person's Google account, they are technically able to open the underlying spreadsheets, which do include reminder content. They only do this when needed to fix a problem or when the law requires it. If you'd rather not store something private in a reminder, keep the reminder text general.

Your information isn't shared with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Reminders are kept so you can see them in My reminders, until your account is deleted.
  • Security records are kept for the most recent 3,000 events, then removed automatically.
  • Password reset codes expire after 15 minutes and are stored only in scrambled form.

Your choices

  • You can edit, pause or delete your reminders at any time in My reminders, including repeating ones.
  • You can turn WhatsApp reminders off, or disconnect WhatsApp, in My reminders.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and its reminders by emailing the address below.
  • If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Reminder Desk's access at any time in your Google Account under Security, then Third-party connections.

Children

Reminder Desk isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
